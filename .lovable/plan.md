

# Update Translation Strings for SEO

Update two translation keys in both English and Dutch localization files to improve SEO keyword density on club profile pages.

---

## Changes

### English (`src/i18n/locales/en/common.json`)

| Key | Before | After |
|-----|--------|-------|
| `trainersAtLocation` | "Trainers at this location" | "Padel trainers at this club" |
| `similarClubs` | "Similar Clubs in {{city}}" | "Other padel clubs in {{city}}" |

### Dutch (`src/i18n/locales/nl/common.json`)

| Key | Before | After |
|-----|--------|-------|
| `trainersAtLocation` | "Trainers op deze locatie" | "Padeltrainers bij deze club" |
| `similarClubs` | "Vergelijkbare Clubs in {{city}}" | "Andere padelclubs in {{city}}" |

---

## SEO Impact

- Adds "padel" keyword to trainer section heading
- Reinforces "padel clubs in [city]" for local search
- Natural language flow maintained in both languages
